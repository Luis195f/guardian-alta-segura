# Problem evidence for the judge narrative

Cut-off: 2026-09-09. Sources were consulted on 2026-09-09. This register
supports only a narrow description of post-discharge phone work: it can be
time-bound, require repeated attempts and still fail to reach some people. It
does not establish that Guardián Alta Segura improves contact, clinical
outcomes, safety, adherence, readmission, workload or cost.

## Permitted synthesis

> Post-discharge follow-up can be a concrete phone-work problem: teams may work
> within short service windows, make repeated attempts and still not reach every
> person. Guardián Alta Segura demonstrates a synthetic, human-controlled way to
> organize two relay intents and their evidence; it has not been evaluated for
> workload, reach or clinical outcomes.

## Wording excluded from the submission

- GAS reduces calls, workload, missed contacts, readmissions, relapse, suicide
  or cost.
- The cited contact rates apply to Spain, a future GAS population or CALL-E.
- A 48-, 72-hour or seven-day value is an approved GAS protocol.
- Telephone contact is required by NICE or is the only valid follow-up method.
- The evidence establishes clinical effectiveness, safety or causal benefit.
